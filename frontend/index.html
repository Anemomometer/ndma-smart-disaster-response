2. Enhanced Entity-Relationship (EER) Concepts Applied

Specialization / Generalization

Disjoint Complete Hierarchy

Disaster_Event is specialized into Natural_Disaster (attributes: meteorological category, wind speed, seismic magnitude, flood depth) and Technological_Disaster (facility name, hazardous material type, containment status). Categorized with total disjoint constraints (a disaster is either natural or technological, never both).

Weak Entities

Existence-Dependent Relations

1. Sensor_Telemetry depends on IoT_Sensor (Partial Key: recorded_at).
2. Dependent_Family_Member depends on Rescued_Individual (Partial Key: dependent_seq). If the owner record is deleted, dependents and telemetry have no independent existence.

Aggregation

Higher-Level Relationship Entity

The relationship between Rescue_Team and Disaster_Event is aggregated into Rescue_Deployment. Resource allocations and logistical supply orders are directly linked to the specific deployment mission rather than floating in a ternary link.

Category / Union Type

Polymorphic Relief Recipient

Relief_Recipient = Relief_Camp ∪ Affected_Community. Relief materials can be consigned to an institutional camp shelter or directly to an isolated village community via a unified foreign key abstraction.

3. Normalization from UNF to 3NF / BCNF

UNF & Anomalies

A single unnormalized DISASTER_DISPATCH_RECORD containing repeating groups of items, evacuees, and teams introduces severe Insertion Anomalies (cannot create camp without victims), Deletion Anomalies (discharging last victim deletes camp info), and Update Anomalies (redundant agency data).

1NF (First Normal Form)

All attributes are rendered strictly atomic. Multivalued fields (specialized equipment, distributed items) are decomposed into separate tuples. Primary keys are defined.

2NF (Second Normal Form)

Eliminated all Partial Functional Dependencies on composite candidate keys. For example, in DISTRIBUTION(DisasterID, CampID, ItemID, DispatchDate, ItemName, Quantity), ItemID -> ItemName was partial and decomposed into RELIEF_ITEM(ItemID, ItemName).

3NF & BCNF

Eliminated all Transitive Dependencies. In RESCUE_TEAM(TeamID, TeamName, AgencyID, AgencyName), TeamID -> AgencyID and AgencyID -> AgencyName was transitive and decomposed into AGENCIES and RESCUE_TEAMS. Every determinant is a candidate key, achieving Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF).

4. Advanced Database Technology Justification: DuckDB

  • Vectorized Columnar Analytical Engine: Disaster coordination is query-intensive; calculating camp occupancies, triage distributions, and rolling time-series alerts across thousands of sensor rows executes in sub-2 milliseconds via SIMD columnar execution.
  • Network Partition & Offline Resilient: In catastrophic situations where external cellular and cloud datacenters are severed, DuckDB operates embedded in-process on mobile command units without heavy client-server daemons.
  • Pure SQL GIS & Spatial Math: Directly computes spherical Haversine distances to match nearest available relief camps and dispatch rescue vehicles.
  • Native ACID Reliability: Ensures zero orphaned records when registering evacuees, dispatching medicine, or allocating emergency beds.
